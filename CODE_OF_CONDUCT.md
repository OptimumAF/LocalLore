# Community conduct

LocalLore should be a welcoming place to build useful software and tell stories. Treat contributors with respect, discuss ideas rather than attacking people, accept constructive criticism, and give credit for others' work. Different backgrounds and skill levels are welcome.

Harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory abuse, publishing private information, sexual harassment, and malicious disruption are not acceptable. Do not post other people's private conversations or story saves. Keep reports and disagreements focused on relevant behavior and evidence.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces formally operated for the project. It does not appoint maintainers as moderators of the entire internet or of private offline stories.

Avery Fernandez (`@OptimumAF`) is the initial enforcement authority. Responses may include a request to edit or remove content, a warning, a temporary participation restriction, or a ban, proportionate to the behavior and available evidence. Where practical, explain the action without revealing confidential information.

For a concern that contains no sensitive details, contact the owner in the relevant issue or pull-request thread. For harassment, threats, or personal-data exposure, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting tools or Support contact form](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). This is the bootstrap route for private abuse reports, including concerns about the owner; it is operated by GitHub and is not a project-owner inbox or a promise that GitHub adjudicates every disagreement under this policy. Do not put sensitive evidence in public issues. For other sensitive project concerns, request a private contact without posting the details. A dedicated project conduct inbox may be added later. Private vulnerability reporting is reserved for security vulnerabilities. See [ADR-0002](docs/adr/0002-bootstrap-reporting.md).

This policy does not promise a staffed response time or an independent appeals committee. The owner is also expected to follow it.
