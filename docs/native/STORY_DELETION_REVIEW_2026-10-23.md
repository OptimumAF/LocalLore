## Observer and authority boundary

The [official SQLite data_version contract](https://www.sqlite.org/pragma.html#pragma_data_version) makes this a per-connection observation. Values from different connections cannot be compared meaningfully; commits on the observed connection itself do not change its value. A distinct retained read-only observer therefore matters: it can notice commits by the original writable Store as well as other owners, including same-content delete/reimport ABA. The observer must remain the exact same connection across preview and confirmation, without a long read transaction pinning a snapshot. Sample around coherent preview construction, then acquire the target writer's BEGIN IMMEDIATE before the final comparison. Any intervening commit may conservatively invalidate consent, including unrelated-story and derived-cache changes. Ordinary busy/error/cancellation paths must consume or retain capability ownership explicitly and drop the observer without partial deletion.

SQLite connection filenames and data_version alone cannot establish that an already-open connection still targets the inode currently at that path after OS-level replacement. The storage author independently raised this limitation. Use canonical guarded path/metadata checks to reject detectable drift and explicitly retain the trusted app-private, nonreplacement boundary; do not claim same-UID filesystem-adversary protection. A foreign database plan must fail before any target table changes. No constructor/schema/epoch change is justified merely by this bounded design.

The [official foreign-key documentation](https://www.sqlite.org/foreignkeys.html) distinguishes immediate checks at statement completion from deferred checks at transaction commit. Schema2's immediate branch-parent references permit deleting every target branch in one statement after its child tables are gone; the story-root reference is deferred and must be resolved by the story deletion before commit. Do not delete branches one at a time or disable foreign keys. Verify the complete namespace is empty and foreign_key_check is clean inside the transaction, preserving all other namespaces and global migration metadata.
